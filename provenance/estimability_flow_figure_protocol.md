# Candidate-dataset estimability flow figure protocol

The supplementary flow figure will visualize the already documented disposition of the 11 candidate datasets in Supplementary Table S2. It will not introduce a new literature-screening denominator or imply a formal systematic review.

The fixed stages are nominal temporal coverage, at least two distinct eligible analytical sample units per group, resolvable sample mapping, retained-cell eligibility where applicable, tissue/estimand compatibility and final contrast estimability. Each dataset will be assigned only from existing audited metadata and Supplementary Table S2. The figure will state that nominal day-1/day-7 coverage does not guarantee estimability of direct day-7-minus-day-1 change.

This is a data-provenance flow figure, not a conceptual mechanism diagram or graphical abstract.
