# GSE205029 candidate temporal-context decision

Date: 2026-08-03

The candidate was evaluated only for eligibility before expression-count download or outcome analysis. The intended role was an independent injury-model context stress test, not an additional study in the existing three-study meta-analysis.

The unchanged project rules required at least 20 retained cells per sample-by-state pseudobulk and at least two eligible biological samples in both contrast groups. The candidate contrasts were wild-type day 1 versus uninjured, wild-type day 7 versus uninjured, and direct wild-type day 7 minus day 1. No statistical significance threshold or pathway result would be used to decide eligibility.

If any required group had fewer than two eligible biological samples, that contrast would be recorded as not estimable. Cells would not be substituted for biological samples, the one-cell library would not be pooled into another mouse, and raw-read reprocessing would not be used selectively to rescue a failed author-filtered sample.

Only NCBI GEO metadata, author-filtered cell metadata, feature identifiers and sample-barcode mappings were inspected for this decision. The expression-count matrix was not downloaded or analyzed.
